How Mutual of Omaha prices Medigap

  • Attained-age pricing. Your premium is based on your current age and rises as you get older.
  • Tobacco status. Non-tobacco rates are usually lower.
  • Location. Premiums can vary by ZIP code and county.
  • Guaranteed renewable. Mutual of Omaha cannot cancel your policy because of your health or claims.

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare Supplement plans does Mutual of Omaha sell in Florida?

Mutual of Omaha sells standardized Medigap plans in Florida, including Plans A, F, G and N. Plan F is open only to people who became eligible for Medicare before January 1, 2020. Ask us for the plans offered at your ZIP code.

Does Mutual of Omaha use attained-age pricing?

Yes. Mutual of Omaha prices Medicare Supplement plans by attained age, so premiums rise as you get older. Rates can also vary by ZIP code and tobacco status, and non-tobacco users usually pay less.

Can I see Mutual of Omaha rates for my ZIP code?

Yes. Enter your ZIP code in the rate tool on this page and it opens Mutual of Omaha's quote for your area. You can also call (855) 847-7020 and an agent will compare it with other Florida carriers.

Do I need a doctor network or referrals?

No. Medigap has no network. You can see any doctor or hospital in the U.S. that accepts Medicare.

Is there a health questionnaire?

Not during your 6-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period, which starts when you are 65 and enrolled in Part B. Outside that window, Mutual of Omaha may ask health questions.

Is the quote free?

Yes. Quotes are free and carry no obligation. Our agents are paid by carriers, not by you.
