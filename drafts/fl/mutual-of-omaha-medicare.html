What Mutual of Omaha Medicare offers in Florida

Mutual of Omaha’s Medicare product in Florida is Medicare Supplement insurance, offered as Plans A, F, G and N. It fills the deductibles, copays and coinsurance that Original Medicare leaves you with.

  • Use any doctor or hospital in the U.S. that accepts Medicare
  • No networks or referrals
  • Guaranteed renewable for as long as you pay your premium
  • Coverage travels with you anywhere in the U.S.
  • Plan F is closed if you became eligible for Medicare after 2019

Medigap or Medicare Advantage?

You cannot have both. Medigap adds to Original Medicare and keeps your doctor choice wide open. Medicare Advantage replaces it and usually uses a network. Our side-by-side guide walks through the trade-offs.

Frequently asked questions

When can I enroll in Mutual of Omaha Medicare Supplement?

Your best window is the 6-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period that starts the month you are 65 or older and enrolled in Part B. During it you cannot be turned down or charged more for health reasons. After it, Mutual of Omaha may ask health questions.

Is Mutual of Omaha a Medicare Advantage plan?

No. The plans on this page are Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans. They work alongside Original Medicare. You cannot use a Medigap plan together with a Medicare Advantage plan.

Do I still need Part A and Part B?

Yes. You must be enrolled in both Medicare Part A and Part B to buy a Medicare Supplement plan, and you keep paying your Part B premium.

Can I switch to Mutual of Omaha later?

Yes, but outside your open enrollment window you may have to answer health questions, and Florida does not guarantee acceptance for most people. Some situations carry guaranteed-issue rights. An agent can check yours.
