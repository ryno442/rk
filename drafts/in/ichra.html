Group renewals keep rising and small groups in Indiana have fewer carrier choices every year. An ICHRA flips the model: you set a fixed monthly allowance, and each employee picks the individual plan that fits them, with the reimbursement tax-free to both of you.

How an ICHRA works

  • You choose an allowance for each class of employee (for example full-time, part-time, or by location)
  • Employees buy individual coverage on or off HealthCare.gov
  • They submit proof of coverage and premiums, and you reimburse up to the allowance
  • Reimbursements are excluded from income and payroll taxes

Things to plan for

  • Affordability. Employers with 50 or more full-time employees must meet ACA affordability tests to avoid penalties.
  • Notice. Employees must get written notice, generally at least 90 days before the plan year starts.
  • Local plan prices. Individual premiums vary by age and county, so we model the allowance against real Indiana rates.
  • Under 50 employees? A QSEHRA is a simpler alternative with annual limits set by the IRS.

Frequently asked questions

What is an ICHRA?

An individual coverage health reimbursement arrangement lets an employer reimburse employees, tax-free, for individual health insurance premiums and, if the employer chooses, other medical expenses.

What size business can offer one?

Any size. Employers can set different allowances for different classes of employees, such as full-time and part-time, or by location.

Do employees have to buy their own plan?

Yes. Each employee must enroll in individual health coverage or Medicare to be reimbursed.

Can employees still get a marketplace subsidy?

Only if they decline the ICHRA and the ICHRA is considered unaffordable for them. If it is affordable, they cannot get a premium tax credit.
