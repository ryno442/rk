Short-term health insurance covers a gap: between jobs, waiting for employer coverage to start, or after missing open enrollment. Coverage can start within days and premiums are often well below full-price ACA plans. The trade-off is real, so here is the honest version.

Rules in Texas

Texas allows short-term plans, but most policies sold in Texas today run only a few months. Ask us what terms are available on your start date.

At the federal level, a 2024 rule limits short-term plans to three months, or four months with a renewal. In August 2025 the federal agencies said they would not prioritize enforcing that limit and would write new rules. As of October 2026 the 2024 rule is still on the books, so call us for the terms carriers are actually offering when you need to start.

Who it fits

  • Healthy people between jobs or waiting on employer coverage
  • People who missed open enrollment and have no qualifying event
  • Early retirees bridging a few months to Medicare
Not a fit if you are pregnant, take ongoing prescriptions, or are treating a condition. Those costs are typically excluded. An ACA plan during open enrollment (November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027) is the safer choice.

Frequently asked questions

Is short-term health insurance ACA coverage?

No. Short-term plans do not have to cover pre-existing conditions or all essential health benefits, and they are not eligible for premium tax credits.

How long can I keep a short-term plan?

Texas allows short-term plans, but most policies sold in Texas today run only a few months. Ask us what terms are available on your start date. A 2024 federal rule limits terms to three months (four with renewal). Federal agencies said in August 2025 they would not prioritize enforcing it and plan new rules, so available terms may change.

Can I buy short-term coverage any time?

Yes. Short-term plans are not limited to open enrollment, and coverage can start within days.

Is there a penalty for having short-term coverage?

There is no federal penalty for going without ACA coverage.

Short-term limited duration insurance is not comprehensive coverage and is not required to comply with federal ACA requirements. It may not cover pre-existing conditions. Read the policy for exclusions and limits.
