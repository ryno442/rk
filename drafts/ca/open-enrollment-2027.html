Open enrollment for 2027 health insurance in California runs November 1, 2026 through January 31, 2027. This year matters more than most: the extra federal subsidies are gone, many carriers filed rate increases, and the rules for paying back subsidies got stricter. Here is what changed and how to avoid a surprise bill.

After open enrollment closes, you generally need a qualifying life event (losing other coverage, moving, marriage, a new baby) to enroll or change plans.

What changed for 2027

  • The enhanced subsidies expired. The larger premium tax credits that ran from 2021 through 2025 ended on December 31, 2025, and Congress had not restored them as of October 2026. The 400% of poverty income limit is back, so some households who had help before will get none in 2027.
  • No cap on paying subsidies back. Starting with 2026 coverage, if your advance credit was too large you repay the full difference when you file taxes. Accurate income estimates now matter a lot.
  • Higher out-of-pocket limits. The most an ACA plan can make you pay in 2027 for in-network covered care is $12,000 for one person and $24,000 for a family.
  • Fewer lawfully present immigrants qualify for credits. Eligibility narrows starting January 1, 2027. If this may affect your household, call us before you enroll.
  • More plans work with an HSA. Bronze and catastrophic plans bought through the marketplace count as HSA-qualified plans starting in 2026. For 2027 the HSA limit is $4,500 for self-only coverage and $9,000 for family coverage (plus $1,000 at age 55 or older). California does not follow federal HSA tax rules, so contributions are not deductible on your California return and earnings are taxed by the state each year.
  • California still has an individual mandate. Going without qualifying coverage can mean a state tax penalty on Form FTB 3853.
Do not just let it auto-renew. If your plan's premium jumped or your subsidy shrank, a different metal level, network or carrier may cost less. We compare every plan we offer in your county, on and off Covered California, in one call.

If you earn too much for a subsidy

Above 400% of the federal poverty level you will pay full price. You still have options: an HSA-qualified Bronze plan to lower your taxes, off-exchange plans, an employer ICHRA if you own a business, and supplemental coverage to fill a high deductible. Frequently asked questions

When is open enrollment for 2027 health insurance in California?

Open enrollment through Covered California runs November 1, 2026 through January 31, 2027. Enroll by December 31, 2026 for coverage that starts January 1, 2027. Deadlines can be extended, so check Covered California or call us.

Did the extra ACA subsidies come back?

Not as of October 2026. The enhanced premium tax credits expired on December 31, 2025. For 2027 coverage, households above 400% of the federal poverty level do not qualify for a premium tax credit, and those below it are expected to pay a larger share of income than they did through 2025.

What happens if my income estimate is wrong?

Starting with 2026 coverage, there is no cap on paying back extra advance premium tax credits at tax time. If your income goes up during the year, report it right away so your credit is adjusted.

Can I keep my current plan?

Many people are renewed automatically, but carriers change networks, drugs and prices every year. Compare before December 31, 2026 so a renewal does not lock you into a worse fit.

Does it cost more to use an agent?

No. Licensed agents are paid by the carrier, and the premium is the same whether you enroll with us, on Covered California, or with the carrier directly.
