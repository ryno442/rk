If you have a Medicare Supplement plan in Illinois and you are between 65 and 75, you get a 45-day window every year, starting on your birthday, to switch to a plan with equal or lesser benefits without medical underwriting. Medigap premiums tend to rise as you age, so this window is worth checking every year.

How the Illinois birthday rule works

  • The window opens on your birthday and lasts 45 days
  • You must be age 65 through 75 and already have a Medigap policy
  • The new plan must have equal or lesser benefits (for example Plan G to Plan G, or Plan G to Plan N)
  • No health questions, so a health condition cannot be used to decline you
  • Insurers must notify eligible policyholders about the window

Frequently asked questions

What is the Illinois Medigap birthday rule?

An annual 45-day window, starting on your birthday, when Illinois Medigap policyholders age 65 to 75 can move to a plan with equal or lesser benefits without medical underwriting.

Who qualifies?

Illinois residents age 65 through 75 who already have a Medicare Supplement policy.

Can I upgrade to a plan with more benefits?

Not under the birthday rule. Moving to richer benefits usually means answering health questions.

Does it apply to Medicare Advantage?

No. The birthday rule is for Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policies only.
