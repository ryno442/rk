If you have a Medicare Supplement plan in Illinois and you are between 65 and 75, you get a 45-day window every year, starting on your birthday, to switch to a plan with equal or lesser benefits from your current insurer or an affiliated company, without medical underwriting. Medigap premiums tend to rise as you age, so this window is worth checking every year.

How the Illinois birthday rule works

  • The window opens on your birthday and lasts 45 days
  • You must be age 65 through 75 and already have a Medigap policy
  • The new plan must have equal or lesser benefits (for example Plan G to Plan G, or Plan G to Plan N)
  • The new policy must come from your current insurer or one of its affiliated companies
  • No health questions, so a health condition cannot be used to decline you
  • Insurers must notify eligible policyholders about the window

Which plans you can move to

The birthday rule lets you move within your insurer's family of companies, not to any insurer. Many Medigap carriers sell through more than one affiliated company, sometimes at different premiums for the same plan letter. We check what your insurer and its affiliates offer and handle the switch so your old policy ends the day the new one starts, with no gap. Moving to an unrelated company is still possible, but outside a guaranteed-issue situation it means answering health questions.

Example: You have Plan G and your premium went up at renewal. In the 45 days after your birthday you can move to Plan N with the same insurer, or to a Plan G from an affiliated company at a lower premium, without health questions. Benefits are standardized, so a Plan G covers the same things whichever company issues it.

What to do before your birthday

  • Note your birthday on the calendar and call us about two weeks before
  • Have your current policy and premium handy
  • We compare your insurer's and its affiliates' Plan G and Plan N premiums for your ZIP code and age
  • Do not cancel your current policy until the new one is approved

Frequently asked questions

What is the Illinois Medigap birthday rule?

An annual 45-day window, starting on your birthday, when Illinois Medigap policyholders age 65 to 75 can move to a plan with equal or lesser benefits from their current insurer or an affiliated company, without medical underwriting.

Who qualifies?

Illinois residents age 65 through 75 who already have a Medicare Supplement policy.

Can I switch to a different insurance company?

Only to an insurer affiliated with your current one. Moving to an unrelated company usually means answering health questions, and the new insurer can decline you.

Can I upgrade to a plan with more benefits?

Not under the birthday rule. Moving to richer benefits usually means answering health questions.

Does it apply to Medicare Advantage?

No. The birthday rule is for Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policies only.
