Final expense insurance is a small whole life policy built for one job: making sure the cost of a funeral and final bills does not fall on your family. Coverage is simple to qualify for, premiums stay level, and the benefit is paid directly to your beneficiary.

How it works

  • Smaller face amounts sized to funeral and end-of-life costs
  • No medical exam on most policies, just a few health questions
  • Premiums are typically locked in and the policy builds cash value
  • Your beneficiary can use the money however they need

Frequently asked questions

What is final expense insurance?

A small whole life policy meant to cover funeral, burial or cremation, and final bills, so your family is not left paying them.

Do I need a medical exam?

Usually not. Most final expense policies use a short health questionnaire instead of an exam.

What if I have health problems?

Graded or guaranteed issue policies are available. They typically pay a limited benefit (often a return of premium plus interest) if death from natural causes happens in the first two or three years.

Does the premium go up?

Whole life final expense premiums are generally fixed for life, and coverage does not expire as long as premiums are paid.

Life insurance products are underwritten by the issuing carrier. Coverage, eligibility, and benefits vary by carrier and state; the policy controls.
